# The Process of Sanctification

Salvation, child, servant, friend, and bride

## The Progression

There are four ways scripture describes our relationship to God. I believe that this is a bit of a subtle description of our walk with Christ. In church lingo, that is called "[[sanctification]]," which just describes the process of refining yourself and [[Be transformed|transforming]] into a more righteous, humble version of yourself. That is, the slow growth toward becoming more like [[Yeshua|Jesus]].

## Child

First, we must be [[Children of freedom|children]]. The prerequisite for this is [[salvation]] and finding your identity as a child of God and co-heir with Christ.

In this stage, we learn to trust God in all things and accept that knowledge is in conflict with that trust. Wisdom is not a bad thing but knowledge gained out of right relationship with God is dangerous - think back to the [[Garden of Eden]]. At best, we become puffed up. At worst, we discard God entirely.

You know you are graduating out of this phase when you find yourself consistently obedient but you're not exactly understanding why things are the way they are. You may also be telling yourself "there's gotta be more," without doubting that God has it under control.

## Servant

The servant is one who has their own autonomy. The servant chooses to submit their will to the master, God. They still may not fully understand [[the why of it all]], but they're starting to get glimpses of it and they're obedient regardless.

A [[bondservant]] ([[Exodus]] 21:2-6) in Scripture is an interesting term. In the Bible, there are really only a handful of classes: slaves, bond servants, day laborers, and masters. The slave who loves his master won't leave when his term ends ([[Jubilee]]). He chooses to stay because he loves his master and his master loves him. His ear is pierced in public view of the community and he serves forever, elevated to a member of family. See also [[be a servant]], [[servants]].

You know you're in this class when your heart and flesh starts to become in conflict with God's will. You know you're moving out of it when your flesh stops persistently winning. Temptation may remain but your heart is so aligned with God that you choose to remain faithful to Him. In this phase, God begins to hand you [[authority]]. You receive the full power of the Holy Spirit and are given some agency. It is also when you're most vulnerable to the enemy and is arguably where most [[Fake Christians|serious Christians]] live the majority of our lives.

## Friend

>[[John]] 15:5-17: "No longer do I call you servants… but I have called you friends."

A friend is pretty easy to define. It's someone you get along with, trust, and spend time with intentionally. In the context of faith, this simply means your heart is aligned with God to the point that each of you genuinely enjoys the others company. You may not always want to hang out with your kids, or even your employees, but you are always game for a nice time with a friend. Further, a friend is who you call after a bad day. From gods vantage point, a friend is someone you trust to walk your dog while you're away.

At this phase, the power you've been given now truly gets flexed. God entrusts to watch over his flock. He asks you to take care of those around you and gives you authority and influence in kind. "Friend of God" is a title we should all strive for.

You know you're in this phase when talking with God through prayer or reading Scripture feels easy and fun. When your heart aches over what hurts God and rejoices over what brings him pleasure. In this life, this is the end goal. In the next, there is one more phase.

## Bride

One of the most common parallels, or [[shadow picture|shadow pictures]], for our relationship with God is that of marriage. In [[Revelation]], we read of the 144,000 and [[the marriage supper]]. This is because we are all invited to become [[144,000#The Bride of Christ|the bride of Christ]] (Rev 14 and Rev 19). [[Matt 21-28]] walks through wedding customs: the groom prepares a place, the father approves, and his friends announce his coming. In our current wandering of [[the Wilderness]] called life, we have faith of this coming reality and hope for things yet to be seen.

We read that, at this phase, we will be put in charge of regions of the [[New Earth]]. Truly on the same level authority as Christ himself (gives a new meaning to co-h
eirs, doesn't it?). At this point, we walk with Jesus daily. Love what he loves. Hate what he hates. Act exactly how he would act. Not because we have to but because we love him. This is the end goal.
