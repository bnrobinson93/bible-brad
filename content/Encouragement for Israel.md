[[Isaiah]] 40 and 41 are beautiful pictures of God being a loving husband. It speaks of how our sins are payed twice over, how those who oppose us will be brought low and how we will be blessed. God above does not grow weary and he is the creator of all. Only cling to him and he will lead us into a life abundant.
