## Commanded

These are required. Neither were instituted to remove intentional sin, only unintentional or accidental ([[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Leviticus|Lev]] 4:2, 4:20, 4:22, 4:27, 5:2-6, [[Numbers|Num]] 15:22-29). There is no animal sacrifice that can forgive intentional sin. [[Numbers|Num]] 15 says that's intentional sin, native or foreign, they must be cut off and their guilt remains. Humbling yourself before God, a [[broken and contrite heart]], and asking for forgiveness is the only way to be forgiven. Grace is the only answer. [[David]] does this with Nathan after Bathsheba [[2 Sam]] 12:12-13 (leading to [[Psalms|Ps]] 51). Same with [[Manasseh]]. [[2-Areas/Bible/Teaching/Micah]] 6:6-8, [[Psalms|Ps]] 40:6-8 tells us what God truly desires: mercy, humility, faithfulness. [[With words, we return to God]].

- Sin
- Guilt / Trespass
