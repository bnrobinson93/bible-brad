In [[1 Cor]] 15,  Paul discusses our new bodies. After speaking of [[Jesus's resurrection]] answering the pressing question of "is [[resurrection]] a thing," ("he answers, I sure hope so or this is all a joke") he tries to answer the question of what that will look like.

He likens the resurrection to a plant. A plant cannot grow unless first the seed of that plant is planted, watered, and ultimately dies. In our Christian walk on this earth, we are the seed; we must be planted in the good soil of the [[Gospel]], watered in righteousness and [[faith]] in [[Jesus]], and ultimately die. Only then can our full potential be revealed in our heavenly body.
