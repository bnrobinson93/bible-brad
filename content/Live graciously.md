# Live graciously
From [[Psalms|Ps]] 50, the LORD owns all and has need of nothing. However, he charges us to give to the ministry and allows us to submit a sacrifice. This is for our benefit and the benefit of those around us. [[Proverbs|Prov]] 22:22-23 says we should not hold the poor as lower being. If you do, the Lord himself with repay any exploitation life for life.
