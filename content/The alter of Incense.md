[[Exodus|Ex]] 30 outlines the spice blend to use. The blend of spices is only to be used in the temple. If it was used elsewhere, the person using it should be cut off from the congregation. This is the **only** blend to be offered. [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Leviticus|Lev]] 10 - Aaron's sons who approach wrongly. They bring "strange fire" and are killed by **God's fire** for it. They offered something of the world, not something of God, essentially [[idolatry]].

[[Deuteronomy|Deut]] 12 - do not worship God how the pagans worship their Gods
