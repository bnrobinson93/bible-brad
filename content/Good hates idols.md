In [[Jeremiah]] 10 and 11, we get a glimpse of God's hatred for idol worship. He mocks the idea of creating something and then treating it as if it were a god and laments at how he had loved both Judah and Israel, only to have them turn harshly from him. He promises all of the curses of the law and their ultimate destruction as penalty.
