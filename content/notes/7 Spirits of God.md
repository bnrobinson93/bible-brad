The spirits are mentioned in both [[Isaiah|Isa]] 11:1-5 and (soft reference) [[Revelation|Rev]] 1:12-20. They are:

1. Spirit of [[Wisdom]]
2. Spirit of [[Understanding]]
3. Spirit of [[Council]]
4. Spirit of [[Sight]]
5. Spirit of [[Knowledge]]
6. Spirit of [[Reverence]]
7. Spirit of the delight in the fear of the [[LORD]]
