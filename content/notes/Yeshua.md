# Yeshua

 In English, Jesus. The name in Hebrew reads Yeshua or Yehoshua, which means "[[LORD|YHWH]] Saves" or "Salvation comes from God." This gives clarity to the passage in which his name is told to [[Mary and Joseph]]. The closest, direct English translation would be "Joshua," which means "Savior."

  >[[Matthew|Matt]] 1:21 ESV
  >She will bear a son, and you shall call his name **Jesus**, for he will **save** his people from their sins."

In Greek, which the [[New Testament]] was written in, the name is transliterated to Iesus - the letter J did not come into existence until the 17th century (prior to that it was always an "I"). We can even see this carry over in the Muslim faith, where the Quran calls Jesus "Isa." In Latin, this became Iesus:

>Matt 1:16 VulgCC
>Iacob autem genuit Ioseph virum Mariæ, de qua natus est **Iesus**, qui vocatur Christus.
>
>(ESV)
>and Jacob the father of Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom **Jesus** was born, who is called Christ.
 
 The lamb of [[God]] who takes away the sins of the world ([[John]] 1:29). jesus
