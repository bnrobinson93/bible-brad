# LORD

The all-caps rendering of the divine name YHVH in English translations. Sometimes referred to as the Tetragrammaton, this name is first revealed in [[Exodus]] 3:13-15 in answer to [[Moses]]'s question "who shall I say sent me." God responds, "I AM that I AM,"אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה אֶהְיֶה. In Hebrew, this literally translates to:

- Haya - "to be" or 
- Ashēr - which/because
- Haya - "to be"

So God responds with, "I exist because I exist. Tell the people of [[Israel]] that **the one who actually exists** has sent you." He then gives his actual name:

>[[Exodus]] 3:15 ESV 
>God also said to Moses, "Say this to the people of Israel: '**The LORD** (יְהוָה, YHVH), the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you.' **This is my name forever**, and thus I am to be remembered throughout all generations.
