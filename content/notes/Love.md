# Love

Right after finishing discussion of the spiritual gifts, in [[1 Corinthians|1 Cor]] 12, Paul says that you could have all of the gifts in the world but without Love, they are meaningless. Of the three most important attributes of a Christian (faith, hope, and love), love is the greatest.

Paul defines love as having these qualities:

- Patient
- Kind
- Does not envy
- Does not boast
- Is not proud
- Does not dishonor others
- Is not self-seeking
- Is not easily angered
- Keeps no record of wrongs
- Does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth
- Always protects
- Always trusts
- Always hopes
- Always perseveres

Therefore, when we read [[1 John]] 4:8, we know that God is all of these things.

>1 John 4:8 ESV
>Anyone who does not love does not know God, because God is love.

God, in his perfect love, exemplifies each of the traits in his son, [[Yeshua|Jesus]], setting aside his Godhood and coming as a servant to all. His faithfulness is true and we know that he sends his [[Holy Spirit]] to protect us, empower us to persevere hardship, and lean on him.

While you could certainly make the case that God does boast and is jealous, his patience, slow-anger, and forgiveness far outweigh these. Only after constant begging does he finally bring [[judgement]]. [[God withholds wrath for his sake|God withholds wrath, and forgives constantly, for his own sake]]. We, being made in his image, should take his example.
