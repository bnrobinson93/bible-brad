# God's Punishment Is Just 

In [[Ezekiel]] 14, God speaks of his judgment and how none except the righteous should be spared. However, he then discusses how he plans to leave a few so that we may see a witness as to how judge the judgment was (v23, "you will know that nothing was done without cause").

In chapters 15 and 16, he describes why he judges Israel. He starts by saying they were like a child of heathens left for dead; they were covered in blood, naked, and still had their umbilical cord hanging off their nearly lifeless body. They squirmed in agony on the side of the road. God came and brought them back to life; He set them up to grow strong. When he returned, he found Israel again naked but this time at an age appropriate for marriage. He again clothed Israel in fine garments and made her His queen. She was known across the world. However, Israel then took the jewelry given to her and made it into idols. Took of her ornate clothes and put it on the idols. She took God's children and sacrificed them to those same idols. As if that weren't enough, she, acting as a  prostitute, insisted on **paying** her adulterous suitor. She stood on every street corner and begged for promiscuity (16:32-34). It is for this reason that God judges them; for this he divorces Israel (v38).

Be reminded of this and live as the one who God has honored. We are saved wretches that were made into rulers. A kingdom of undeserving priests. Honor God as he has already honored you! Do not seek after the world - don't even allow the world to seek after *you* - but love God.
