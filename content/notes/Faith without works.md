# Faith without Works

In [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/James|James]] 1, we are reminded to not only learn about God but to actually do what he says. Likewise, in James 2, a comparison is drawn between wishing well for someone but doing nothing to help in their time of need. Remember also that [[faith]] is more accurately understood by the English word "faithful." The word itself, in Hebrew, is related to the word "mother," which I take as a form of [[shadow picture]]: faith is exemplified by a mother's sacrificial, selfless love as shown to her family minute-to-minute, day-after-day. With that in mind, [[faith]] without works is no faith at all. We must **show** that we trust Him and His word by living out our faith our actions.
