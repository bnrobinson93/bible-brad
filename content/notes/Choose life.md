# Choose Life

In [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Leviticus|Leviticus]] 25 and 26 (paralleled in [[Deuteronomy]] 7 and 8), God lays out his exact plan for us if we are faithful to him or if we turn away.  In the case of faithfulness, he fights our battles, gives us so much that we can't possibly use all of the gifts, and protects us from negative world events that directly impact everyone else. If we turn away, however, he gets progressively more angry, allowing us to fall to disease, driving wild animals to attack us, allowing other nations to conquer our land. Ultimately, He says he will pile our lifeless corpses on the very idols we prayed to.

God then reminds us that all of this is up to us. He says that if we turn away but the realize our sin that he will welcome us back with open arms. There will still be [[consequence]] but he **will** return us safely to him.

This is reiterated later in [[Deuteronomy]] 28-30 where he ends with "today I set before you life and death, blessings and curses. Therefore, choose life!"
