title: God is Slow to Judge
updated: 2025-01-23T21:55:33-06:00
date: 2025-01-23T21:55:33-06:00
---

In [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/2 Peter|2 Peter]] 3, we are reminded that God is coming to judge. People will scoff, saying he is never coming, but we must remember that time is different with God. He will delay because he wants all to be saved, and he is patiently giving us as much time as possible to achieve that mission. For God, a day is as a thousand years and a thousand years is as a day, time is fluid. Is he really waiting _that_ long in that case?
