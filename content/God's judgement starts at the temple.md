We see an interesting vision in [[Ezekiel]] 8-9. The vision begins with a show of all the debauchery occuring in Israel - idols in the temple, leaders worshiping idols in secret, women in the outer court of the temple weeping for [[Tammuz]], and men facing away from the temple and worshiping the sun. Then it turns to God. Six men enter with deadly weapons and among them is essentially a travelling scribe. God's presence is described to stand up off of his throne in the [[Most Holy Place|Holy of Holies]] and step to the tent's entry. From there, he commands the writer to mark any who weep for the sins of the nation. Any who did not have a mark were to be slain. The group go forth immediately and do as commanded.

This is a chilling reminder to us. We are not to allow the world to taint us. Judgement begins from the temple and when God is provoked, his judgement is fierce and swift.
