In [[2 Thessalonians|2 Thess]], Paul talks about the end of times. He says that the day will only come "after the rebellion" and the man of [[lawlessness]] is revealed. He will denounce all other gods and proclaim himself to be God, setting up in the temple. He will use Satan's power and authority to deceive. Ultimately, those who fail to love the Truth and will be deluded out of believing in salvation from God through Jesus.
