Gold is the first element mentioned in the bible ([[Genesis|Gen]] 2:11-12). God calls it 'good'

Solomon's temple equates to roughly $220B in gold and another $22B of silver. There was too much bronze to weigh so it is estimated to have had roughly $260B of rare elements.
