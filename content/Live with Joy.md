# Live with Joy
In [[2 Corinthians|2 Cor]] 6, Paul speaks of how God is with us in the day of salvation - which is today! Despite how badly the world will treat us, even unto death, we are called to love, sing and praise, rejoice, and give all we have to others to further the Kingdom.
