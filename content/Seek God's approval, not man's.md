---
title: Seek God's approval, Not Man's
updated: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
date: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
---

In [[Gal]] 1, Paul rhetorically asks whether he seeks the approval of man or of God. He answers in saying that, if he were seeking the approval of man, he would not be a servant of Christ. In so saying, he teaches that we are not to conform to this world but should only concern ourselves with running a good race for Christ. We answer to God alone, the opinion of a man does not matter an ounce so long as our soul is in question.
