The number 7 is very substantial in the bible. It is generally considered to represent "perfection" or "completion," largely coming from the idea that on the 7th day of creation, God considered all creation to be completed.

A few places where we see this number:
- 7 days per week, with the 7th day, itself, being the [[Sabbath]]
- 7 days of [[Creation]]
- 7 [[Feasts of the LORD]] 
- 7 lights on the [[menorah]]
- 7 [[attributes of God]]
- 7 eyes that see the earth (Rev 5:6)
- [[7 Spirits of God]]
- 7 Churches in Asia ([[Revelation]] 1-4)
- 7 horns & 7 eyes ([[Revelation|Rev]] 5:6)
- 7 thunders ([[Revelation|Rev]] 8:2)
- 7 trumpets ([[Revelation|Rev]] 10:3-4)
- 7 crowns on the dragon ([[Revelation|Rev]] 12:3)
- 7,000 people die ([[Revelation|Rev]] 11:13)
- 7 Bowls of wrath ([[Revelation|Rev]] 15:7, 16:1)
- 7 Plagues of wrath ([[Revelation|Rev]] 15:1, 8, 16:1-21)
- 7 Mountains ([[Revelation|Rev]] 17:9)
- 7 days, priests, and marches around Jericho ([[Joshua|Josh]] 6)
