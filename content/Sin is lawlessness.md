title: Sin is Lawlessness
updated: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
date: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
---

In [[1 John]] 3, we see that sin is defined as being without law. Jesus came and perfectly lived out the law and we are told to emulate him. In fact, it states that if we continue to sin, we don't know Jesus at all, we haven't even seen him. Rather, he takes our sins upon himself and we should refrain from breaking the law (sinning) from the moment we are saved.
