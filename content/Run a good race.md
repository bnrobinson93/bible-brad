title: Run a Good Race
updated: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
date: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
---

In [[1 Corinthians|1 Cor]] 9 and 10, Paul says we should run a race like someone training to win. Don't run aimlessly. Don't get hit and then sit down. Run like you want to get the crown we get to actually keep: the one in heaven!

He then compares against what happens to Israel in the wilderness and demonstrate how God holds us accountable to the race we're running.
