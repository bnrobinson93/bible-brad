Also called the lampstand. It was made of solid gold and had 7 flames which were to be tended to continually ([[Lev]] 24:4). We see years of this icon as it was highly important to the working of the [[temple]] and was, in fact, instructed by God to be placed in the temple.

![[Pasted image 20240804085856.png]]

The menorah was the only light source in the sanctuary. We see the fulfillment of this - or what it was a shadow of - when Jesus proclaims himself (on [[Hanukah]]) to be the light of the world ([[John]] 8:12). However, Jesus also calls **us** the light of the world ([[Matt]] 5:14-16) when we show our good works ([[righteousness]]). Just as the menorah was to be tended continually, we must tend continually to our spiritual righteousness.

> [!summary]
> Israel, full of the spirit of the LORD, is the light of the world that goes into the darkness. The righteousness of Israel is something that requires constant tending.
