---
title: Do Not Associate with Unrepentant Sinners
updated: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
date: 2025-01-23T21:55:32-06:00
---

In [[1 Corinthians]] 5 Paul is clear that a brother who is unrepentant of his sexual immorality is to be excommunicated. There is no room in the church for someone who is unrepentant and to keep them in. Our midst is proud of us and presumptuous. He is clear that we should not treat the world this way, but only those who call themselves brothers and sisters in Christ. The world is to be judged not by us but by God. Christians are to be held accountable to each other.
