# Being Busy

## Context

- It was hard to find time to do anything outside of just work, eating, and sleeping
- All of us have been in a similar situation
  - This has been a busy week!

## What Does the Bible Say about Being Busy?

  Do not be conformed to this present world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may test and approve what is the will of [[God]] - what is good and well-pleasing and perfect.

## Tying it together

- We should not allow ourselves to be swept away by the world. We are to be renewed in our minds each day and test and approve of what God’s will is for our life
- [[Yeshua|Jesus]] himself withdrew in times when it was difficult to even eat because it was so busy. We should emulate this behavior
- While difficult, our [[God]] doesn’t respond to loud shouts and is not found in wind, fire, landslides, or earthquakes but in a still, small voice in the wind. We should be attuned to his voice in our day-to-day life
- We see a non-example in the story of Mary and Martha:
  - Martha is busy readying a feast for Jesus, important work for an important man
  - Mary sits at [[Yeshua|Jesus]] feet, not anxious at all, and listens to him
  - When Martha confronts [[Yeshua|Jesus]] and Mary, he responds by saying it’s better to be there with him than to fuss about this world: “call on him while he is near!”
