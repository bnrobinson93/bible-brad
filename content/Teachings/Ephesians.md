# Ephesians

## Context

- [[Ephesians]] was written while Paul was in Roman prison around 60AD
- [[Ephesians]] was a coastal town that Paul had visited - and stayed for anywhere from three months to about two years - earlier in his ministry (see [[Acts]] 19)
- This was written to the church in Ephesus, several of which members Paul would have known well

## The Gospel Story

**Who** and **what** we are in Christ, **where** we’re going **when** it’s all over, and **why** we’re going there

### Blessings (1)

- Paul opens with a forever long run on sentence about God’s holiness and how we’re adopted into his family
- [[God]] has provided his redeption and forgiveness of our sins to us through His blood, by grace
- This grace, unifies all of heaven and earth to [[God]] and God’s will
- Our response to the gospel grants us access to the Holy Spirit, which is the “down payment” of our inheritance - salvation and life everlasting

### Prayer (2:1-10)

- Pauls prayer is wordy but boils down to wanting us to know [[God]] more
- Knowign God, provides hope, joy, and strengthens our faith, leading to blessing and an outpouring of the same same power that raised Christ from the dead

### Death to Life

- We are dead in sin
- God’s mercy and [[Love]] has made us alive in Christ
- We have been saved by grace through faith as a gift from God, not from works (2:8-9)
- As a new creation in Christ, we are made for good works (v10), so walk in them
- That is: we are saved by grace but our behavior should change

### One Messiah (2:11)

- Those that were not a part of Israel are part of the family
- There is no Jew or Greek, only Christian

### One New Man (2:14)

- [[Yeshua|Jesus]] is our peace, removing the fence of laws in favor of spirit
- [[Yeshua|Jesus]] nullified the traditions to make two - Jew and Greek - into one (Isa 44:5, Ezek 34:24, 37:17,24, [[John]] 10:16, Rom 11:17-24)
- Having become one, there is no longer a separation of man, we are to be unified with [[Yeshua|Jesus]] as the cornerstone

### Paul’s Ministry (3)

- Paul sits in awe of his mission: to reach the “heathens” on behalf of Jesus
- His ministry is to call heathens to become fellow heirs and members of the body of Christ, sharing in Jesus’s promises
- He’s called to preach the gospel, bring to light mysteries / reveal God’s wisdom, and, therefore, give hope and confidence to believers

### To Know the [[Love]] of Messiah (3:14)

- Because of this ministry, I’m imprisoned, but don’t worry, I’m praying for His spirit to empower you, to strengthen your hearts through faith, and to become rooted in [[Love]] such that you can understand the fullness of God

> [!important]  
> Question: Does Paul’s description of his mission or the Gospel change how you view them? Would you share the gospel in the same way?

## Our Story (Therefore…)

### The Unity of the Body (4)

- Walk in humility and gentleness, patience, and love, peaceful, and unified in body and spirit
- Become mature in Christ: growing, joining, uniting together with [[Yeshua|Jesus]] as the head
- [[Yeshua|Jesus]] is growing the body, built up in love

### The Old Life and the New (4:17)

- Don’t work futily
- Lay aside the old, destructful, decietful desires
- Allow the renewal of the spirit in: that of righteousness, holiness, and truth

### Rules for the New Life (4:25-5:5)

- Speak truthfully
- Be angry but don’t [[sin]] (the sun must not set on your anger)
- Do not steal
- Advise and encourage (”rotton” words must never leave your mouth)
- Do not offend the holy spirit - respect God
- Rid yourself of bitterness, impulse, anger, business, and blashpemy
- Be kind, compassionate, forgiving, loving, christ-like
- Do not speak fouly, foolishly, or punch-down
- Be thankful
- Do not worship idols

### Walk as Children of the Light (5)

- Do not be lead astray: you are now children of the light
- Live a changed life, don’t allow grace to become license
- Expose darkness
- Redeem your time for the days are evil - understand the will of God
- Speak in psalms and sing praises, play music from your heart, and give thanks to God

## Examples

### Wives and Husbands (5:21)

| **Wife - church**     | **Husbands - Jesus** |
| --------------------- | -------------------- |
| Allow husband to lead | Be the head          |
| Submit to husband     | Be loving            |
| Be pure               | Die on her behalf    |

### Children and Parents (6)

| **Children** | **Fathers**        |
| ------------ | ------------------ |
| Obey         | Do not Provoke     |
| Honor        | Train and instruct |

### Slaves and Masters (6:5)

| **Slaves**             | **Masters**                  |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Obey with respect      | Do the same things as slaves |
| Remain devoted         | Do not threaten              |
| Serve enthusiastically | Do not be partial            |

### The Battle against Evil (6:10)

- Be strong in God
- Be clothed in the armor of God:
  - Belt of truth
  - Breastplate of righteousness
  - Sandles of the gospel of peace
  - Shield of faith
  - Helmet of salvation
  - Sword of the spirit (the word)
- Pray and ask for perseverance
- Be an ambassador in chains, speak boldly
