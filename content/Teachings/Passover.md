# Passover

## Timeline

- 10th day = enter the city (palm Sunday; [[Luke]] 19:28-41)
- 14th day = inspection day (Jesus declared blameless; [[John]] 19:4)
- 15th day = Passover lamb is slaughtered
- first fruits = graves opened and brought to [[God]] (Matt 27:45-54; [[John]] 20:17,27)
- Pentacost/feast of weeks = Mt Sinai; 40 days w/ Jesus; giving of the holy spirit ([[Acts]] 1:3,12-26–2:4)
