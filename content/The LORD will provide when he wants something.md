In [[Exodus]] 37 and 38, we get a detailed description of the articles of the temple. One thing that stands out is how everything was either good plated or solid gold. This was not a small amount, as it is tallied to nearly 200 talents between gold, silver, and bronze. Estimated, this would have been anywhere from $258,000 up to $1,134,000 USD worth of precious metals ((1000-30,000 * 29 talents of gold) + (1940 * 100 talents of silver) + (500-1000 * 70 talents of bronze)). Almost all of this was plundered from Egypt ([[Exodus|Ex]] 12:35-36). God provided them the means before ever asking for anything in return! So, too, will be do with us.
