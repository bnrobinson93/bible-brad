[[James]] 4 is a good reminder that we should humble ourselves before God. We should not ask with the greedy intent of using a gift for worldly gain, nor should we slander others or speak presumptuously about our future plans. Rather, we should recall that we are but a mere speck of dust in the galaxy of time. God wants to spend time with us and is gracious to those that love him, so love him back!
