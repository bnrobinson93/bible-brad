In [[1 Corinthians|1 Cor]] 9, Paul says he is a slave to all. He makes himself as those around him for the sake of the gospel. This does not mean he openly signed while with sinners but, rather, that he approached people with their own words and thoughts. He met people where they were and did not act as if they were weak in their faith just because he was strong in his.
