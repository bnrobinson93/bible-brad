# Give as you'd like to receive
In [[2 Corinthians|2 Cor]] 8, Paul encourages the people to give with excitement. He gives two reasons for this. Firstly, we have the opportunity to emulate Christ, who gave all of his riches and became poor that we may be saved. Secondly, the goal of giving is equality. We give such that others, who do not have, can have. Likewise, when we do not have, others give that we may have. All should have enough to get by.
