In [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Hosea|Hosea]] 2:11, we see that God revokes the feasts as a punishment for the adultery of the Israelites. This is listed along with things like holding back wine, grain, and wool. God considers his "meodim" - his appointed times - as something cherished. He wants to meet with us but if we stub him enough, he stops showing up. Don't stand up God! We see in [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Zechariah|Zechariah]] that the feasts return in the end of times, and [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Hosea|Hosea]] 3 confirms that, though the Jews will spend a time without a king, they will return to him.
