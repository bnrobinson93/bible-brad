Several of the words picked in the King James version were chosen with the explicit intent of making use of "ecclesiastical" terms. The [[protestant|protestants]] preferred words like "congregation" or "washing" while the [[Anglican|Anglicans]] opted for "[[Church]]" and "[[baptism]]," respectively. The [[Puritan|Anglicans]] wanted to shift even **further** as they completely rejected the [[Pope]], and even man, having any say in what the church service looked like (they used "assembly" in place of "[[Church"]]). Ultimately, the [[Anglican]] verbiage won.
