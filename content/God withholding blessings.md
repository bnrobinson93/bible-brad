In [[2 Samuel]] 21, we find that there is a drought in [[Israel]]. [[David]] inquires of the [[LORD]] and is told that it is because of [[Saul's]] betrayal of the [[Amorites]] ([[Gibeon]]), who they had sworn to protect. [[David]] made recompense with them and the rain fell.

While this story may not feel directly applicable, think back on relationships you've betrayed, deals you have broken, or promises lost. [[God]] takes these all seriously (let your yes be yes and your no be no) and will withhold blessing from you until made right. My view is that there is a table, full of gifts and blessings, set before you at your birth. In life, you choose to throw them away - one by one - or you choose to accept them. [[Choose life]]!
