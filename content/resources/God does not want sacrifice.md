[[Isaiah]] 1:12 echos the idea of [[Psalms]], by lamenting over how people were sinning and then sacrificing on repeat.

Likewise, [[Jeremiah]] 7 shows God becoming annoyed by worthless sacrifices. He reminds them that he didn't just give commandments about sacrifice but also called them to love Him!
