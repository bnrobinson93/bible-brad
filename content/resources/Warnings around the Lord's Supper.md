In [[1 Corinthians|1 Cor]] 11, Paul warns that we should come into the Lord's Supper in a manner that is right. He speaks specifically to how some were coming while hungry and, therefore, eating so much of the Lord's Supper that others could not partake at all, causing division and punishing those who could not eat ahead of time.

Paul proclaims that God will judge us in this way, as we bring it in ourselves. All that we have, we should share and we should consider our hearts before partaking in the body and blood of our [[Messiah]].
