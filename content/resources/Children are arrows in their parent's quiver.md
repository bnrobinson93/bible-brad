> [[[Psa]] 127:4 ESV] Like arrows in the hand of a warrior are the children of one’s youth.

This can take two facets. As children, we are called to be sharp tools that our parents send out into the world - sharp in the sense of the Word. As parents, we are to treasure our children, seeing them as a limited and valuable resource that God has gifted to us. An archer does not waste his arrows by shooting them frivolously. As parents, we should direct our children toward paths where they will be of effect. Aim them at their target and they will not miss.
