In [[1 Peter]] 3, he continues his discussion on righteous living. Peter reminds us that being punished for living a good life is rewarding, not a curse. Being punished for evil is only natural. We are not to return evil for evil or insult for insult. We are to live humbly, with compassion, and be constantly reminded of Jesus who went before us. Be holy as he is holy
