# Joshua is renamed

> [[Numbers]] 13:3-4
> And Moses by the commandment of the LORD sent them from the wilderness of Paran: all those men were heads of the children of Israel.
> ...
> [v8] Of the tribe of Ephraim, **Oshea** the son of Nun.
> [v16] These _are_ the names of the men which Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses called Oshea the son of Nun Jehoshua.

Moses renames Oshea ("he saves") to Joshua ("Ya[^1] saves")

Likewise, Jesus greets the women in the garden with "Yahovah[^1] Saves!"
