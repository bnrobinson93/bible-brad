# Spiritual milk and meat

In [[1 Peter]] 2, Peter explains that we must be like babies, constantly hungry for the "milk" of God's word. This will gradually cause us to grow into mature Christians, ready for meat ([[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Hebrews|Hebrews]] 5). Just as a baby bird opens it's mouth wide or a human infant turns it's head at the incline of food, we must be famished for God's word.
