# Follow Jesus's example

[[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Ephesians|Eph]] 5 calls us to walk in love until death, as Jesus did. Sacrifice of self is a [[fragrant offering]] to God. He goes on to say that a life marked with any of the following is not a life touched by God. Obtaining from these does not give you salvation but a transformed life naturally leads to these things being avoided.

- [[sexual immorality]]
- [[impurity]]
- [[greed]]
- [[obscenity]]
- [[foolish talk]]
- [[coarse joking]]

Rather, live a life full of [[thanksgiving]].

> ⁶ Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of such things God’s wrath comes on those who are disobedient. ⁷ Therefore do not be partners with them.
