# Strong and Courageous

Being strong and courageous is a theme echoed all throughout scripture. We see it in:

1. [[Deuteronomy|Deut]] 31:6-7
2. [[Deuteronomy|Deut]] 31:23
3. [[Joshua|Josh]] 1:6-9
4. [[Joshua|Josh]] 10:25
5. [[1 Chronicles|1 Chron]] 22:13
6. [[1 Chronicles|1 Chron]] 28:20
7. [[2 Chronicles|2 Chron]] 32:7
8. [[1 Corinthians|1 Cor]] 16:13

We see in [[Revelation]] 21 that cowards are listed among the sinners, being people who quickly turn from God, spineless. We should be meek, not weak. The weak are cowards but the meek inherit the earth ([[Matthew]] 5:5, [[Psalms]] 37:11); be a warrior in a garden not a gardener in a war; do not react, act.

What the scriptures are saying is to know the time and place to destroy the wicked but **choose** to keep the sword sheathed (unlike Peter in [[John]] 18:10-11). This is not weakness, it is strength. Recall that the tongue is a sword; so we should exhibit self-control not just of our body but of our words, for words come from our heart and can defile us.
