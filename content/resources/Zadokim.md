# Zadokim

In [[Ezekiel]] 38, the measurements of the land in the new earth are given. When it comes to Levi, there is now a portion given, unlike before - when they served in the lands of the brothers, surrendering their 12th to the [[half tribes]]. In the new earth, the Zadokim are the priests and they serve in the temple. These appear to be a race less people and the only requirement is that they were the ones who remained loyal to God. Just outside of that lies the old priestly line, the Levites, but they are forbidden to do commerce nearby. On the outskirts of the square area (25,000×25,000 cubbits) allotted to the priests are homes, farms, and shops.
