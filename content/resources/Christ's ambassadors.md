# Christ's ambassadors

[[2 Cor]] outlines that we are dead to our former selves. We are called to speak on behalf of the one who died in our place and to call all to the saving grace he offered us. He died and, just as [[Adam]] caused all to sin, caused us all to die to [[sin]]. We are now a new creation, one that is a [[bondservant]] to God.
