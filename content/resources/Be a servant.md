# Be a servant
In [[[[Philippians|Phil]] 1, Paul calls us to live a life of servitude both to others and to Christ, living out our mission via service to others and allowing that service to lead them back to Christ. He reminds us that Jesus, despite being equal with God, made himself not only a man but a man who served and ultimately died the humiliating death of a hated criminal. We, too, should serve in this manner, until our dying breath.
