# Dead to the law

[[Galatians|Gal]] 3 reminds us that the law is that which convicts us to death. Jesus came to become the curse [of death] so that we no longer must pay for the sins we commit. Recall "the wages of sin is death" [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Romans|Rom]] but Jesus has paid the price fully for us.
