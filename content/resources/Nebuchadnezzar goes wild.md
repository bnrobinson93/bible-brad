[[Daniel]] 4 is written by King Nebuchadnezzar. It recounts how he knew that Daniel could interpret dreams. He has a new dream that needs interpretation. In this one, he sees a massive tree that gives shelter to all; however, and angel commands it be cut down, yet left alive. Daniel tells him that the king is the tree and that he must repent. One year later, it comes true and the king is driven to madness. He lives in the fields for 7 years, eating and sleeping like a beast. After 7 years, his sanity is restored and he praises God, saying all that God does it right. 

Try to live life in that one year span! Repent and give God praise lest he humble you. In the end, he will be praised so choose life!
