In [[Luke]] 14:7-35, we see a bit of God's heart. One of the disciples states that those who attend the [[marriage supper of the lamb]] are blessed. Jesus, having just discussed picking for yourself the most humble place and inviting in those who have no way of repaying you, repeats that those who count themselves blessed enough to be on the list will manage somehow to be "too busy" to attend. Rather, those who are humble and recognize that they have nothing to offer will be the ones who attend.

Do not be too busy for God. When he calls, answer, and follow without hesitation. The last thing you want is to be one of the invited who loses his seat to a stranger because the host is offended. Carry your cross. Despise this earth. Choose life.
