# Works and law

In [[Galatians|Gal]] 2, Paul asks how a God that saves us from our flesh, the law of sin and death, and the world would expect us to then live a life of flesh and worldliness. We are called to love a spirit filled life, one that counts on [[grace]] and [[faith]] in Jesus, alone, for salvation
