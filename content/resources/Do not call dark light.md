# Do not call dark light

[[Isaiah]] 5:20 says pities the one that calls light "dark" and dark "light;" the one who calls evil "good" and good "evil." The remainder of the chapter outlines their punishment. They are haughty and will be made humble. God will not stand for it.

In the new testament, Paul reiterates this in [[2 Corinthians|2 Cor]] 11:

> 13 the people are false apostles, deceitful Workers, masquerading as apostles of Christ. 14 And no wonder, for Satan himself masquerades as an angel if **his** servants also masquerade as servants of righteousness. Their end will be what their actions deserve.

Don't be a servant to Satan! He only ever accuses!
