# The power of prayer

In [[james]] 5, Elijah is mentioned in the context of prayer, specifically mentioning the halted rain for 3.5 years as a result of his prayer. He encourages us to forgive one another, prayer for each other, be patient on the Lord, judge sparingly, and not to swear.
