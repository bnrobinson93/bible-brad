# The Tongue

The tongue is a two-edged sword, both blessing and cursing others. In [[Matthew]] 15:18 and [[Luke]] 6:45, we see that our words reflect our heart. He goes so far as to say that our words will be what are judged and condemned by ([[Matthew|Matt]] 12:37). [[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/Romans|Rom]] 10:10 echoes this, saying that our words reflect our heart, which leads to salvation.
