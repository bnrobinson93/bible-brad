> [[Proverbs]] 19:26
26 Whoever robs their father and drives out their mother
is a child who brings shame and disgrace.

The words here in Hebrew mean to destroy or devastate ones father and to cause they're mother to flee.

It should be our goal to honor our parents, so that they feel blessed by being our parents. We are, after all, [[Children are arrows in their parent's quiver]].
