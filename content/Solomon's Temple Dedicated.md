The dedication of Solomon's Temple in [[1 Kings]] 8 is interesting. He kneels before the temple and raises his hands to the sky. He then prays for God to recognize this as a home on earth, despite knowing that God cannot be contained in heaven, much less earth. He asks that any who looks toward the temple with a contrite heart be heard of God and forgiven. This sets the stage for [[Manasseh]] later on, as we see that God makes good on this. God always seeks repentance over sacrifice.
