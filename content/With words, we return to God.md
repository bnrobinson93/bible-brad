[[2-Areas/Bible/Teaching/Hosea|Hosea]] 14:1-4

With words return to God. Nothing can save you but God and his forgiveness. He will heal and love us always. This is God's [[grace]], that we are saved without merit and without physical sacrifice.

The literal translation is "to offer our lips as sacrifice to bulls"
