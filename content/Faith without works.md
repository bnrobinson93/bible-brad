In[[2-Areas/Bible/Books of Bible/James|James]] 1, we are reminded to not only learn about his but to do what he says. Likewise, in James 2, a comparison is drawn between wishing well for someone but doing nothing to help; faith without works is no faith at all. We must show that we trust Him and His word by our actions.
